What We Do

Fabrication Services

Serving Internal Campus Departments (Tier 1), Student Clubs (Tier 2), and External Community Businesses (Tier 3).

Rapid Prototyping

Transform your digital models into physical objects quickly. Ideal for validating dimensions, fit testing, and low-fidelity functional proof-of-concepts.

  • Fast Turnaround
  • Structural Integrity Checks
  • Multiple Material Options

CAD Design

Need a custom part but don't have the file? Our trained members will model replacement brackets, specialized enclosures, or functional mechanisms from scratch.

  • Parametric Modeling
  • Reverse Engineering
  • Design for Additive Mfg (DfAM)

3D Scanning

Digitize real-world objects. We provide high-resolution scans for archiving, modification, or duplicating existing parts that are hard to source.

  • High Accuracy Mesh
  • Post-Processing & Cleanup
  • Export to standard formats
